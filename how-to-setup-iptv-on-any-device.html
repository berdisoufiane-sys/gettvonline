Getting started with IPTV can seem daunting, but with the right guidance, it’s a breeze. This guide will walk you through setting up your IPTV service on a wide range of popular devices.

What You'll Need

  • An active IPTV subscription from a provider like GetTV.online.
  • Your M3U link or Xtream Codes login details.
  • A compatible device connected to the internet.

Setup on Amazon Firestick / Fire TV

The Firestick is one of the most popular devices for IPTV. We recommend using an app like IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate.

  1. From the Firestick home screen, go to Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options and turn on "Apps from Unknown Sources".
  2. Install the "Downloader" app from the Amazon Appstore.
  3. Open Downloader and enter the download code for your chosen IPTV app.
  4. Once installed, open the app and log in with your subscription details.
