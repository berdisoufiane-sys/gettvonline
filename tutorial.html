How to Setup Your Device

Android TV & Amazon Firestick

  1. Go to your App Store or use the "Downloader" app.
  2. Download a compatible player (e.g., TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro).
  3. Open the app and select "Login with Xtream Codes API".
  4. Enter the Username, Password, and Portal URL we emailed you.
  5. Click Login and wait for the categories to load.

Samsung & LG Smart TVs

  1. Open your TV's App Store.
  2. Search for and install "IBO Player" or "Flix IPTV".
  3. Open the app and note the "Device MAC Address" displayed on the screen.
  4. Provide this MAC address to our support team, or log into the app's website to upload your M3U link.
  5. Restart the app on your TV.
